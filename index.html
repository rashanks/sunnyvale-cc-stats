How the boards work
Season MVP
Impact points, added up match by match. One point is roughly one run of value, so batting, bowling and fielding share one scale. This follows the idea behind ESPNcricinfo's Smart Stats and CricViz's Match Impact, simplified to what CricClubs records.
Batting points
Runs × a pace factor. The pace factor is the square root of your strike rate divided by that match's overall strike rate, kept between — and —. So 40 off 20 counts for more than 40 off 45, and a fast 30 on a slow pitch counts for more than on a flat one.
Bowling points
Each wicket is worth — if the batter batted in the top 4, — at 5–7, and — at 8–11. You also get runs saved: what an average bowler in that match would have conceded from your balls, minus what you actually conceded. An expensive spell can go negative.
Fielding points
— per catch (keeper catches included), — per stumping or direct run-out, and — for a run-out assist.
Top 3 and trophies
Gold trophy for 1st, silver trophy for 2nd, bronze medal for 3rd. Anyone tied with a podium place gets the same award, so a card can show more than three names. Highest score ranks single innings, so one player can hold two spots.
Minimums
Rate boards only include players with enough of a sample: — balls faced for batting rates and — overs for bowling rates. Both grow with matches played (— balls and — overs per match), capped at — balls and — overs. Busiest batter uses a fixed minimum of — balls, and Bowling dot ball % a fixed minimum of — overs.
Busiest batter
Dot balls faced (no runs off the bat, leg-byes included) per 6 balls faced, counted from the ball-by-ball commentary.
Pace setter
Your runs minus what the team's average strike rate would have scored from the same balls.
Runs saved
The team's economy rate minus yours, times your overs.
Boundary hitter
Balls faced divided by fours plus sixes.
Cleanest bowler
Wides plus no-balls, per over bowled.
Match notes
Written each week from the CricClubs scorecard, ball-by-ball commentary and players' T20 records: a short report in each result, and a detailed report behind "Detailed cricinfo report". The impact player of the match comes from the same impact points.
