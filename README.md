# Sunnyvale CC leaderboards

Season leaderboards, T20 impact stats and match reports for Sunnyvale CC in the NCCA 2026 Winter T20 PB, Division 2.

**Live site:** https://rashanks.github.io/sunnyvale-cc-stats/

## How it works
- `index.html` is the whole page. `assets/dash-lite.js` loads the files in `data/` and works out every board in the browser.
- `data/` holds the season tables and the match-by-match records (innings, spells, fielding, results and match reports), taken from the public scorecards and ball-by-ball commentary on [nccacricket.org](https://www.nccacricket.org) (CricClubs).
- The data files are updated every Monday evening, Pacific time.

## Corrections

- **Nov 1 vs GSCC Bears:** the wicketkeeper's catch the scorecard gives Bharadwaj Jagannath was taken by Govardhan Mucharla.
